# Homolog Alignment Showcase Adapter Notes

This recipe targets **chimerax**.

## Structured Steps
- **Open the conformers and apply distinct coloring.**: Bring in both structures and make them visually distinct.
- **Run matchmaker and focus the moving region.**: Superpose the models and focus the mobile lid region for comparison.
- **Jump between tile and aligned views, then export.**: Show the presentation preset, create a tiled comparison, then export the aligned view.
