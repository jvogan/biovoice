# Homolog Alignment Showcase

Open open and closed adenylate kinase conformers in ChimeraX, align them with matchmaker, tile and compare views, and store a presentation export.

- App: `chimerax`
- Difficulty: `operator`
- Estimated time: `8 minutes`
- Voice mode: `push_to_talk`
- Last verified: `2026-04-04`

## Sample Data
- **Adenylate kinase open**: ./examples/data/local/1ake.pdb
- **Adenylate kinase closed**: ./examples/data/local/4ake.pdb

## What Success Looks Like
- Both models are loaded.
- Matchmaker alignment succeeds.
- Views move between tiled and aligned states.
- An export is saved.
