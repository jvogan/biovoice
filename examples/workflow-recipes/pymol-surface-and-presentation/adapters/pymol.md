# Surface and Presentation View Adapter Notes

This recipe targets **pymol**.

## Structured Steps
- **Load hemoglobin and apply distinct chain colors.**: Open the structure, show cartoons, and color each chain cleanly.
- **Add the surface and place chain labels.**: Wrap a transparent surface over the tetramer and label each chain center.
- **Frame the hero shot and store the scene.**: Orient the tetramer, apply the presentation preset, and store the hero scene before exporting.
- **Export the final hemoglobin presentation PNG.**: Recall the stored hero scene and write the final high-resolution PNG after the framing settles.

## Notes
- The voice console compiles these recipe steps into structured tool calls.
- The manual command list in `recipe.md` is the direct fallback if you want to run the workflow without voice.
