# Binding Pocket Story Adapter Notes

This recipe targets **pymol**.

## Structured Steps
- **Load the structure and establish the visual baseline.**: Bring 1HSG into PyMOL, switch to cartoon plus sticks, and apply clear colors.
- **Focus the active site and call out the catalytic residues.**: Select the ligand neighborhood, zoom the pocket, and label the catalytic aspartates.
- **Measure the pocket and add a transparent surface.**: Add key measurements, lay a transparent surface over the pocket, and store a scene.

## Notes
- The voice console compiles these recipe steps into structured tool calls.
- The manual command list in `recipe.md` is the direct fallback if you want to run the workflow without voice.
