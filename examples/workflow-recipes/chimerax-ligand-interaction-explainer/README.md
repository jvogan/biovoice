# Ligand Interaction Explainer

Load a ligand-bound structure in ChimeraX, build a transparent pocket surface, and compute hydrogen bonds and clashes around the ligand.

- App: `chimerax`
- Difficulty: `intro`
- Estimated time: `6 minutes`
- Voice mode: `push_to_talk`
- Last verified: `2026-04-04`

## Sample Data
- **HIV protease with inhibitor**: ./examples/data/local/1hsg.pdb

## What Success Looks Like
- 1HSG is open in ChimeraX.
- Ligand and pocket surface are visible.
- Hydrogen bonds and clash/contact analysis are displayed.
