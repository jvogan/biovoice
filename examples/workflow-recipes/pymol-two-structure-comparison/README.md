# Two-Structure Comparison

Load open and closed adenylate kinase conformations, align them, isolate the mobile lid, and store paired scenes.

- App: `pymol`
- Difficulty: `operator`
- Estimated time: `8 minutes`
- Voice mode: `push_to_talk`
- Last verified: `2026-04-04`

## Sample Data
- **Adenylate kinase open**: ./examples/data/local/1ake.pdb
- **Adenylate kinase closed**: ./examples/data/local/4ake.pdb

## What Success Looks Like
- Both adenylate kinase models are loaded.
- 4AKE is aligned onto 1AKE.
- The mobile lid is isolated and emphasized.
- Comparison scenes are stored.
