# Two-Structure Comparison Adapter Notes

This recipe targets **pymol**.

## Structured Steps
- **Load both conformations and establish distinct colors.**: Bring both structures into PyMOL and color them for quick comparison.
- **Align the pair and isolate the moving lid.**: Superpose the two models and spotlight the lid region that moves during closure.
- **Store the overview and close-up scenes.**: Save both the global alignment and the lid close-up as named scenes.

## Notes
- The voice console compiles these recipe steps into structured tool calls.
- The manual command list in `recipe.md` is the direct fallback if you want to run the workflow without voice.
